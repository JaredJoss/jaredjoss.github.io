## Selected Research (LLMs & Healthcare)
- **From Text to Voice (RTCA Workshop @ NeurIPS 2026, Oral)**: Replays the same clinical interruption in text and through a live cascaded voice pipeline, and finds that one or two words committed after the patient starts speaking can flip the safety verdict.
  - Link: https://openreview.net/forum?id=ez6dvTCMby
- **Does the Voice Matter? (ML4H 2026 Proceedings; GenAI4Health Workshop @ NeurIPS 2026)**: Tests per-turn frustration detection on real calls from a deployed clinical voice agent. About 59% of patient turns are one or two words, where transcript-only detection does not exclude chance, and whether audio helps depends on the model and the instruction.
  - Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21001
- **Same Eye, Different Scanner (VLM4RWD Workshop @ NeurIPS 2026)**: Benchmarks whether frontier VLMs and OCT foundation models keep the same glaucoma decision for the same eye when the scanner changes, scoring decision flips, sensitivity change and calibration.
  - Link: https://openreview.net/forum?id=1I9Pfkv8dY
- **The Camera, the Split, and the Label (TAE Workshop @ NeurIPS 2026)**: Audits glaucoma-progression results on the public GRAPE benchmark with three checks: a device-only baseline, a patient-grouped split and an input-label overlap test. At this sample size, none of the reported numbers can be distinguished from a trivial baseline.
  - Link: https://openreview.net/forum?id=AJbpzv4COc
- **When Patients Cut In (EMNLP 2026 Findings; oral at DAIH Workshop @ COLM 2026)**: Evaluates whether clinical voice agents can recover when patients interrupt mid-utterance, using conversation-analytic interruption categories and LLM judges to score recovery against required clinical content.
  - Link: https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-emnlp.2456/
- **When the Language Changes, Is It Still Safe? (DAIH Workshop @ COLM 2026)**: Adapts MATRIX to English, French, Polish and Urdu. Emergency handling holds in all four languages, while symptom coverage and follow-up degrade unevenly across models and languages.
  - Link: https://openreview.net/forum?id=mfgdFCAplv
- **WER is Unaware (IWSDS 2026, Oral)**: Finds WER poorly reflects clinical risk from ASR errors and proposes an LLM judge matching clinician safety assessments.
  - Link: https://aclanthology.org/2026.iwsds-1.39/
  - Code: https://github.com/Ufonia/wer-is-unaware
- **MATRIX (arXiv preprint, 2025)**: Structured multi-agent framework to evaluate safety and behaviour risks in clinical dialogue agents at scale.
  - Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.19163
- **Insights from the Inverse (COLM 2025)**: Reconstructing LLM training goals through Inverse RL. Achieved 80.4% accuracy in reward model extraction.
  - Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.12491
- **ASTRID (ACL 2025)**: Automated evaluation framework for RAG-based Clinical Question Answering systems.
  - Link: https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-acl.857/
